SF Bay Area Satellite & RF Ecosystem Directory
This curated directory maps the engineering landscape of modern satellite communications, focusing on the specialized expertise required for high-performance space hardware.

🏙️ San Francisco "Satellite Row" Hub
San Francisco has emerged as a primary cluster for the NewSpace hardware renaissance.

SF Anchors
Company	Focus
Astranis	Specializes in micro-GEO satellites with a heavy focus on software-defined RF payloads and high-power Solid State Power Amplifiers (SSPAs).
Planet Labs	A massive SF-based fleet operator with significant talent in payload communications and hardware systems.
Capella Space	Industry leaders in Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR), possessing an elite pool of microwave and radar front-end engineering talent.
Rising Stars
Company	Focus
Aptos Orbital	A San Francisco-based hardware startup focused on developing space-hardened communications terminals.
Varda Space	Specializes in spacecraft hardware, including integrated systems and RF subsystems.
Albedo & Samara Aerospace	Key players in the expanding SF ecosystem focusing on high-resolution imaging and satellite bus precision.
🇺🇸 The Broader U.S. Hardware Ecosystem
The directory highlights 50+ key players across the country driving innovation in several critical technical niches.

⚡ SSPA Designers & RF Component Leaders
SpaceX (Starlink) — Recognized as the gold standard for high-volume RF payload production, SSPA design, and phased arrays.
MACOM & Qorvo — Leading providers of GaN microwave transistors and high-reliability RF components essential for SSPA signal gain.
Northrop Grumman — An elite hub for microwave talent, specifically in phased array and SSPA design at their Space Park facility.
Raytheon Technologies — Pioneers in high-power RF and GaN-based hardware.
📡 Phased Array & SDR Experts
CesiumAstro — Specialized in active phased arrays and Software Defined Radio (SDR).
Anduril Industries — Employs engineers focused on SDR and Electronic Warfare (EW).
🛰️ Specialized RF Innovators
Amazon (Project Kuiper) — Deep expertise in LEO broadband hardware and advanced RF simulation.
Swissto12 — Innovating with 3D-printed RF components and satellite antennas.
Muon Space — Building satellite fleets with a high-density pool of payload and RF talent.
🔧 Deep Tech & Specialized Manufacturing
The list also includes the critical supply chain for space-grade hardware.

Advanced Materials
Arlon EMD and Rogers Corporation — Provide the high-temperature RF laminates required for spacecraft environments.
Precision PCB & Assembly
Green Circuits and GS Swiss PCB — Specialize in high-density interconnects (HDI) and space-grade PCB assembly.
Thermal Management
Firan Technology (FTG) — Leads in cooling high-power amplifiers through metal-core technology.
